## The Problem
Many learners finish German A1 coursework and book their Goethe or ÖSD exam without knowing if they can actually pass—risking ₹7,500–₹11,000+ in exam fees and weeks of delay on their career/visa journey abroad.

## How "Readiness" Is Calculated (3-Layer Model)
Instead of a naive percentage score that gives false confidence, this diagnostic uses a **3-Layer Exam Fee Protection Model**:
1. **Confidence Discounting ("I'm guessing" toggle):** Confident correct answers earn `1.0 pt`; correct answers marked as a guess earn `0.5 pt` so lucky guesses don't inflate readiness.
2. **Weighted Skill Pillars:** Vocabulary (`30%`) + Grammar (`35%`) + Reading (`35%`).
3. **Safety Buffer & Skill Floor:** Official Goethe/ÖSD passing mark is `60%`. To account for exam-day anxiety, **Ready to Book** requires `>= 78%` overall **AND** `>= 60%` in every individual skill.

## Product Trade-offs & V2 Roadmap
- **Zero Login Friction:** Learners get immediate diagnostic value without an account wall.
- **Honest Blind-Spot Self-Audit:** Highlights that a 5-minute web check cannot test Listening (*Hören*) or Speaking (*Sprechen*) and includes a 2-step pre-booking self-check.
- **Micro-Topic Action Plan:** Maps every question to a specific exam trap (e.g., *Akkusativ negation*, *V2 word order*) to generate 20-minute drills.
- **Next Steps (V2):** Add 3 short audio comprehension clips for *Hören*, native-language grammar explanations, and WhatsApp follow-up tracking to validate predictions against actual exam pass/fail outcomes.
